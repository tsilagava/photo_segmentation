Pet segmentation

Cut a cat or a dog out of a photo. Two U-Nets with a ResNet-34 encoder (trained on Oxford-IIIT Pet, pet IoU 0.9775 on the test set) run in your browser: your photo is never uploaded. Code: github.com/tsilagava/photo_segmentation
